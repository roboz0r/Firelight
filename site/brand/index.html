Logo concepts

A warm campfire identity: rounded flames, logs, embers and glow, deliberately unlike Lit's faceted geometric flame. Every concept is shown at favicon, header and hero sizes, in colour and in one colour (currentColor), on light and dark backgrounds regardless of the system theme. Dev-only page, not part of the production build.

Round 2: Element

Refining round 1's Element (a soft flame between rounded angle brackets). Its problem: the brackets took most of the width, so the flame was the middle third of the square and became a speck at 16–32 px. Each variant below is a different answer to that, keeping the flame held in a tag, the soft round shapes and the warm palette. Files are in concepts/round2/; the -small files are favicon versions.
